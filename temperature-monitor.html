Individual project / Embedded electronics · 2025

Room temperature
monitor.

A battery-powered Arduino monitor with a live temperature display and audible and visual alerts when the reading falls outside a programmed range.

Arduino UnoTMP36CADSoldering

Individual project · January – May 2025

Electronics
and enclosure design.

I designed, soldered, and programmed the monitor, combining a TMP36 analog temperature sensor with an Arduino Uno and a 16×2 LCD. The Arduino converts the sensor voltage to Celsius and updates the display every two seconds.

The programmed alarm range is 26–32 °C. Outside that range, a red LED flashes and a piezo buzzer sounds. A green LED indicates power, and a rocker switch turns the 9 V battery supply on and off.

Enclosure assembly

I planned the component layout in CAD and fitted the electronics into an ABS enclosure. A laser-cut mounting plate supports the Arduino, and a 3D-printed holder secures the battery. Soldered connections and heat-shrink tubing keep the wiring together inside the box.

Proposed improvements

The prototype integrated temperature sensing, an LCD, and threshold alarms in a standalone unit. Proposed improvements include a smaller enclosure, longer battery life, and protection for the exposed temperature sensor.
